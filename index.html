Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is IV therapy safe?

Yes, IV therapy is safe when administered by our certified professionals, following a thorough health assessment to ensure compatibility with your health status.

How long does an IV therapy session last?

Typically sessions last between 45 and 90 minutes, depending on your specific treatment plan and your body's response. Allow up to 2.5 hours for the full visit.

Will I experience any side effects after the treatment?

Most clients have no issues; however, minor side effects can include bruising at the injection site or a cooling sensation during the infusion.

How soon will I notice the effects of the IV therapy?

Many clients feel an immediate improvement in their overall well-being, but individual experiences with the onset of effects may vary.

How frequently should I come in for IV therapy?

The frequency varies per individual needs and goals, which we'll determine together during your initial consultation and ongoing assessments.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept a variety of payment methods to make it convenient for you: cash, debit card, credit card, and HSA.
