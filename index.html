Frequently asked questions

Is IV therapy safe?

Yes, when given by certified professionals after a health assessment to confirm it suits your health status.

How long does a session last?

Typically 45 to 90 minutes of infusion, depending on your treatment plan and how your body responds. Allow up to 2.5 hours for the full visit.

Will I have side effects?

Most clients have no issues. Minor effects can include bruising at the injection site or a cooling sensation during the infusion.

How soon will I notice results?

Many clients feel better right away, but individual experiences vary.

How often should I get IV therapy?

It depends on your needs and goals. We decide this together during your consultation and follow-up.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, debit card, credit card, and HSA.
